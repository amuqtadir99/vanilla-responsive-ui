About Acme Studio

A remote team of 24 designers, engineers and researchers across nine time zones.

Our story

We started in 2016 rebuilding public-sector services that had to work for everyone, on every device. That discipline — plain web standards, measurable accessibility, no unnecessary dependencies — now shapes everything we ship.

Today we work with startups and enterprises on web apps, design systems and AI assistants.

  1. 2016 Founded to rebuild public services
  2. 2019 Launched our open design system
  3. 2023 First AI assistant in production
  4. 2026 120 products launched

Values and accessibility statement

  • Everyone, every device

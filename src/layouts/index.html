All layouts

  • Stacked — Full-width sections. Home and marketing pages.
  • Sidebar left — Section nav beside content. Services, help, settings.
  • Sidebar right — Content with a right column. Blogs and listings.
  • Holy grail — Nav, content and aside. Portals and catalogues.
  • Docs — Sticky nav, readable width, table of contents.
  • Split — Two equal halves. Contact pages, product details.
  • Centered — One narrow column. Sign in, checkout, forms.
  • App shell — Top bar, sidebar, content. Dashboards and AI apps.

Each layout is a complete, valid page in src/layouts/. Use them directly, copy their markup, or create pages with node .claude/skills/pages.js new. See docs/LAYOUTS.md.
